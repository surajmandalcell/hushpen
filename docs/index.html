Hushpen 2.0.0

Private dictation on your own computer

Hold a key and speak. Hushpen transcribes your speech locally, cleans up the text, and pastes it into the app you are using. Your audio and text never leave the machine unless you choose an endpoint yourself.

hold to talk Right Option on macOS, Right Alt on Linux by default. You can record your own key.
local models Whisper models from tiny to large-v3-turbo. They download only when you ask, with a checked hash.
paste Hushpen saves your clipboard and restores it. It never types into a secure field.
cleanup Rule-based cleanup is on by default. AI cleanup is off until you turn it on.

features

What it does

  • Command Mode: hold a key, speak an instruction, and the model edits the selected text
  • Dictionary: your own words and replacements
  • History: search, re-paste, reprocess, delete with undo, and export to TXT, SRT, VTT, and JSON
  • Import: transcribe audio files with a queue and cancel
  • AI cleanup: a local model, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint such as Ollama or LM Studio
