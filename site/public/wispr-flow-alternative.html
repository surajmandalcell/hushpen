Which one fits you.

Wispr Flow is a polished product with strengths Hushpen does not have yet. Here is an honest split.

Choose Wispr Flow if

  • You need dictation on an iPhone or Android phone today.
  • You want a meeting Notetaker in the same app.
  • Your company needs a team plan, admin controls, or SOC 2 and ISO 27001 reports.
  • You want 100+ languages and no model to download.

Choose Hushpen if

  • You want free dictation with no word limit and no subscription.
  • You want your voice to be transcribed on your own computer.
  • You work offline, on a plane, or on poor Wi-Fi.
  • You use Linux, or you want to read and change the source code.

Switch from Wispr Flow in three steps.

  1. Download HushpenGet the free app for Mac, Windows or Linux. There is no account to create.
  2. Pick a speech modelHushpen asks once. Parakeet v3 is the recommended model, at 416 MB. It downloads only when you ask.
  3. Press the key and talkOn Mac, hold Right Option to talk, or press Option+Tab for hands-free. The text appears in the app you are using.

Add your names and terms in the Dictionary, or turn on a word pack, so they come out spelled your way.

Questions.

Is there a free alternative to Wispr Flow?

Yes. Hushpen is free and open source, with no word limit, no subscription and no account. It runs speech to text on your own computer. Wispr Flow also has a free plan, with a weekly word limit.

Does Hushpen work offline, unlike Wispr Flow?

Yes. Hushpen transcribes on your computer, so it works with Wi-Fi off. You need the internet only to download a model. Wispr Flow's Data Controls page says its transcription always occurs on the cloud.

Which languages does Hushpen hear compared with Wispr Flow?

Parakeet v3, the recommended model, hears English and 24 other European languages. Whisper Large v3 Turbo adds Hindi and many more. Wispr Flow lists 100+ languages.

Does Hushpen have an iPhone or Android keyboard like Wispr Flow?

Not yet. The iOS and Android apps are coming soon, and the iPhone app will come to TestFlight first. Wispr Flow lists Mac, Windows, iOS and Android today.
