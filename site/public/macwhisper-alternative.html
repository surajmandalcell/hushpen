MacWhisper alternative that is free and open source.

MacWhisper and Hushpen both run Whisper on your own computer. MacWhisper is built around transcribing audio files. Hushpen is built around dictation: press a key, talk, and the text appears in any app. It also transcribes files, and it runs on Windows and Linux too.

Which one fits you.

If you transcribe interviews all day, MacWhisper has tools Hushpen does not. If you dictate all day, read on.

Choose MacWhisper if

  • You transcribe many interviews, podcasts or meetings, and you want speaker recognition.
  • You need watch folders, batch jobs, subtitles or YouTube links.
  • You only use a Mac, and you prefer a one-time payment.
  • You want an iPhone and iPad app today.

Choose Hushpen if

  • You want to dictate into any app every day, for free.
  • You use Windows or Linux as well as Mac.
  • You want open source code you can read and change.
  • You want AI cleanup that fixes grammar and punctuation with a local model.

Try Hushpen in three steps.

  1. Download HushpenGet the free app for Mac, Windows or Linux. There is no account to create.
  2. Pick a speech modelWhisper Large v3 Turbo, Small and Base are there, and Parakeet v3 is the recommended model. Models download only when you ask.
  3. Dictate or drop a fileHold Right Option and talk, or open Manual and drop audio files into the queue.

Hushpen and MacWhisper can sit side by side. Use MacWhisper for long recordings and Hushpen for everyday dictation.

Questions.

Is Hushpen a free alternative to MacWhisper?

Yes, for dictation and for transcribing audio files. Hushpen is free and open source. MacWhisper has a free version, and MacWhisper Pro is a one-time payment.

Does Hushpen run Whisper on my computer like MacWhisper?

Yes. Hushpen runs Whisper Large v3 Turbo, Small and Base with whisper.cpp on your computer, and it runs Parakeet v3. Your audio is not sent to a cloud service for transcription.

Can Hushpen transcribe audio files like MacWhisper?

Yes. Manual takes audio files, keeps them in a queue, and exports TXT, SRT, VTT and JSON. It does not have the speaker recognition, watch folders or YouTube links that MacWhisper Pro lists.

Does Hushpen work on Windows and Linux?

Yes. Hushpen runs on Windows x64 (preview) and on Linux x64 and arm64, as well as on Mac with Apple silicon. MacWhisper is a Mac app, with an iPhone and iPad app.
