Voice typing apps turn your speech into text in any app. They differ in where your audio goes, how you pay, and which devices they run on. This page compares Hushpen with five popular apps, using what each one says on its own site.

How to choose.

Three questions settle most of it.

  • Where should your voice go?Hushpen and MacWhisper transcribe on your computer, and Superwhisper has local models too. Wispr Flow and Aqua Voice use cloud servers. Willow runs in the cloud and lists an offline mode.
  • How do you want to pay?Hushpen is free with no limits. Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, Aqua Voice and Willow have free plans with limits and paid plans. MacWhisper Pro is a one-time payment.
  • Which devices do you use?All six run on Mac. Hushpen also runs on Windows and Linux, and it is the only one of the six that lists Linux. Wispr Flow, Superwhisper and Aqua Voice list iOS and Android. Willow lists iPhone.

Questions.

What is the best free dictation app?

It depends on what you need. Hushpen is free with no word limit, no subscription and no account, and it is open source. Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, Aqua Voice and Willow have free plans with limits, and MacWhisper has a free version. Compare where your audio goes and which devices you use.

Which dictation apps work offline?

Hushpen runs speech models on your computer, so it works offline. MacWhisper runs local models on your Mac. Superwhisper says it works offline, and Willow lists an offline mode. Wispr Flow says its transcription always occurs on the cloud.

Is there an open source dictation app?

Yes. Hushpen is open source under the AGPL-3.0-or-later license, and its code is on GitHub. The other apps in this comparison do not list an open-source license.

Which dictation apps work on Linux?

Hushpen runs on Linux x64 and arm64 with an X11 session. Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, MacWhisper, Aqua Voice and Willow list Mac, Windows, iOS or Android, and none lists Linux.

What is voice typing?

Voice typing, also called dictation or speech to text, means you talk and an app types the words for you. Some apps also clean up the text, for example by removing filler words and fixing punctuation.
