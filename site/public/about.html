About Hushpen. Free dictation for Mac, Windows and Linux.

Press a shortcut, talk, and Hushpen types clean text into any app. Speech turns into text on your own computer, so it works offline and your voice stays on your own devices. Android and iOS apps are coming soon.

Hushpen is free and open source under AGPL-3.0. There is no subscription, no account, and no word limit.

It is made by Suraj Mandal.
