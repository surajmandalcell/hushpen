Descriptions

One line

Hushpen is free, open source dictation for Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone and iPad. Speech to text runs on your own device.

Short

Hushpen turns speech into clean text in any app. It works offline, because speech recognition runs on your own device and your voice never goes to a cloud service. It needs no account and no subscription. It is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later and made by Suraj Mandal.

Long

Hushpen is a dictation app. Press a shortcut on a computer, or tap the microphone key on the iPhone and iPad keyboard, talk, and Hushpen types clean text into the app you are using. It removes filler words, fixes capitals and spacing, and understands spoken punctuation. A personal dictionary teaches it your names and terms. Speech recognition runs on the device with Parakeet v3 and Whisper models, so Hushpen works offline and your voice stays on your own devices. There is no account, no analytics and no word limit. Your iPhone or iPad can also serve its models to your own computer over an encrypted local connection. Hushpen is free and open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later. It is made by Suraj Mandal.
