Voice typing in any app.

Hushpen works wherever you can type: mail, chat, notes, a code editor, a browser. It removes fillers, repeats and take-backs, and you can turn on AI cleanup that rewrites the text for where it goes.

  1. Press the keyOn Mac, hold Right Option to talk, or press Option+Tab for hands-free. On Linux and Windows, hold Right Alt to talk.
  2. TalkA small bar shows that Hushpen is listening. Press the key again, or let go, to finish. Esc cancels.
  3. The text appearsClean text lands in the app you are using. History keeps every dictation, so you can search it, play it back and export it.

Offline Whisper and Parakeet.

Hushpen runs speech models on your computer. Whisper models run through whisper.cpp, an open source program that runs OpenAI's Whisper on ordinary computers. You download a model once, and only when you ask. After that, dictation needs no internet.

Questions.

Is there free speech to text for Mac?

Yes. Hushpen is a free, open source dictation app for Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 13 or later. It types what you say into any app, and it has no subscription, no account and no word limit.

Is there free offline speech to text for Windows?

Yes. Hushpen runs on Windows x64 as a preview, with no admin rights needed. Speech models run on your computer, so dictation works offline after you download a model.

Does speech to text work offline on Linux?

Yes. Hushpen runs on Linux x64 and arm64 with an X11 session and a Vulkan or OpenGL driver. On Wayland, the global key and paste are not available. Dictation itself runs on your computer.

Can I use Hushpen for speech to text on iPhone or Android?

Not yet. The iOS and Android apps are coming soon, and the iPhone app will come to TestFlight first. The Android app is a keyboard with a voice bar, and dictation runs on the phone.

What is whisper.cpp?

whisper.cpp is an open source program that runs OpenAI's Whisper speech models on ordinary computers. Hushpen uses it to run the Whisper models, so speech turns into text on your own machine.
