Which one fits you.

Willow has an iPhone app, a writing feature and team controls that Hushpen does not have yet. Here is an honest split.

Choose Willow if

  • You need dictation on an iPhone today.
  • You want Scribe to draft writing from a short spoken request.
  • Your team needs SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA support or SSO.
  • You want 100+ languages and no model to download.

Choose Hushpen if

  • You want every feature free, with no Pro plan to buy.
  • You want speech to text to always run on your computer, not only in an offline mode.
  • You use Linux, which Willow does not list.
  • You want open source code you can read and change.

Try Hushpen in three steps.

  1. Download HushpenGet the free app for Mac, Windows or Linux. There is no account to create.
  2. Pick a speech modelHushpen asks once. Parakeet v3 is the recommended model, at 416 MB. It downloads only when you ask.
  3. Press the key and talkOn Mac, hold Right Option to talk, or press Option+Tab for hands-free. The text appears in the app you are using.

Questions.

Is there a free alternative to Willow Voice?

Yes. Hushpen is free and open source, with no paid plan. Willow's Basic plan is free too, with a weaker speech model and 20 Scribe uses a week.

Does Hushpen work offline like Willow's offline mode?

Yes. Hushpen always runs speech to text on your computer, so it works offline all the time, not in a separate mode. You need the internet only to download a model.

Does Hushpen have a feature like Willow Scribe?

Hushpen has AI cleanup, which rewrites what you said for a profile such as Message, Email or Notes, using a local model or your own endpoint. It cleans up your dictation. It is not a writing assistant like Scribe.

Which systems does Hushpen support compared with Willow?

Hushpen runs on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows x64 (preview), and Linux x64 and arm64. The iOS and Android apps are coming soon. Willow lists Mac, Windows and iPhone.
