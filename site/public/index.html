Use models on your other devices.

Let a phone or a server do the work. Everything stays on your own devices.

Coming soon
  1. 1
    Pair a deviceScan a QR code (nearby, encrypted with Noise), or connect over SSH or Tailscale.
  2. 2
    Pick where models runTurn on Use models on other devices. Speech to Text and Text cleanup each list devices in order, with latency and status.
  3. 3
    Dictate as usualThe first device that answers does the work. This computer is the fallback.
  • Your phoneAndroid serves in the background or with the app open. iOS serves in Server mode, with the app open, charging, and the screen dimmed.
  • Your serverhushpen-host is a headless program for servers. Pair it over SSH or Tailscale.
  • No cloudAudio and text move only between your own devices. Or choose Do not load models on this computer.
