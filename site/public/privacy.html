Privacy policy. Hushpen collects nothing that identifies you.

Last updated 12 November 2026. This policy covers the Hushpen apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Linux, and this website.

Hushpen has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Speech recognition runs on your own device. The developer receives no personal data from the apps. Update checks send the app name, platform, and version. We count them per day and keep nothing that identifies you.

What stays on your device

  • Your voice. Hushpen records the microphone only while you dictate. On iPhone and iPad the recording is a temporary file in the app's private storage. Hushpen deletes it when the dictation ends, and at the latest when the app next opens.
  • Your text. Dictated text, dictionary words and settings stay on the device. On iPhone and iPad the keyboard and the app pass the finished text to each other through an App Group, a private container that only the two parts of Hushpen can read. A result expires after ten minutes.
  • History. On Mac, Windows and Linux, History keeps your transcripts, and the recordings if you keep audio, in the app's data folder on your computer. You can delete one entry or all of them.
  • Pairing keys. The keys of devices you pair are stored on those devices.

What uses the network

  • Model downloads. When you tap Download, Hushpen fetches a speech or voice model from huggingface.co. Hushpen checks each file against a fixed SHA-256 checksum. It sends no account, identifier or content. Like any web host, Hugging Face sees the address of the request.
  • Updates on Mac, Windows and Linux. The app asks github.com for a signed update notice. Update checks send the app name, platform, and version. We count them per day and keep nothing that identifies you. Updates on iPhone and iPad come from the App Store; that app does not check for updates itself.
  • Your own devices. Pairing and Server Mode connect devices you choose, over your local network, or on a computer over your own Tailscale or SSH link. The connection uses Noise encryption. No Hushpen server takes part.
  • AI cleanup on a computer. This is off until you enter a server address in Settings. Then Hushpen sends the dictated text to that server. Choose a server you trust; this policy cannot cover it.

iPhone and iPad permissions

  • Microphone. Records your speech after you tap the microphone key or the Record button.
  • Keyboard and Full Access. iOS requires Full Access before a keyboard can receive the text that Hushpen made. The keyboard never records, and it has no network code. To insert text safely it reads the text around the cursor, only to check that the field did not change. It does not store or send that text.
  • Camera. Scans the QR code that pairs your computer. Hushpen does not save the images.
  • Local network. Lets Server Mode and pairing reach your own computer.
  • Background audio. Lets a recording continue after you go back to the app you were typing in.
  • Live Activity. Shows that Hushpen is listening on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It shows no text.
  • Apple system model. Optional text cleanup on devices that support it. It runs on the device through Apple's framework, and it is off until you turn it on.

What Hushpen does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising or crash-reporting code in the apps, and no analytics beyond the anonymous update-check count above.
  • No tracking, and no selling or sharing of data. The apps send the developer nothing that identifies you.
  • The apps are not directed at children, and they collect no data from anyone, including children.

This website

The website is static pages served by Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. The download buttons read the latest release number through the site's own server, which caches release data from GitHub and stores nothing about visitors. Like any host, Cloudflare processes standard request logs, such as the IP address and browser, to deliver and protect the site.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything the apps keep is on your device, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Deleting Hushpen from an iPhone or iPad removes its models, settings and pairing keys. On a computer, delete entries in History, or delete the app's data folder; About shows where it is. To withdraw a permission, turn it off in your system settings. To forget a paired computer, use Devices in Hushpen.

Third parties

Hushpen relies on three outside services. Hugging Face hosts the model files. GitHub hosts the source code, the issue tracker and the update notices. Cloudflare hosts this website. No third-party code inside the apps collects data. The libraries the apps use are listed under Open-source licenses in the app and on GitHub.

Check it yourself

Hushpen is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Everything on this page can be checked in the source code.

Changes

A change to this policy appears on this page with a new date. The full history is public in the project's repository.
