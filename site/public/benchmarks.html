Benchmarks.

Dictation apps and speech models on the same Mac and the same recordings. Last run 19 November 2026.

  • 95.0words right out of 100 for Hushpen + Whisper Turbo, the most accurate.
  • 0.73 sto write out 1 minute of audio with Hushpen + Parakeet v3, the default model.
  • 19English accents in 36 scored TED clips, scored against human captions.

Results, the best pick for each need.

Words right is 100 minus the word error rate. Seconds per minute is how long it takes to write out one minute of audio. Lower is faster.

  1. Most accurate
    Hushpen + Whisper Turbo95.0 words right out of 100. 2.76 s per minute of audio.

    Next best: Handy with 94.7 words right.

  2. Fast and accurate
    Hushpen + Parakeet v393.6 words right out of 100. 0.73 s per minute of audio.

    Fastest app: FluidVoice with 0.37 s per minute and 93.5 words right.

  3. Balanced
    Hushpen + Parakeet v3 + S1-mini cleanup94.5 words right out of 100. Cleanup adds about 0.3 s to each 25 s clip.

    Beside it: Handy with 94.7 and FluidVoice with 93.5 words right.

Long recordings.

One hour of audio took 16.7 s in FluidVoice, 55.0 s in Hushpen with Parakeet v3, and 184.9 s in Hushpen with Whisper Turbo. The clips were looped into one file of each length.

Text cleanup with S1-mini.

Hushpen can clean up the text with a small local model, S1-mini. It lifted Parakeet v3 from 93.6 to 94.5 words right, and Nemotron Unified from 94.2 to 94.5, and added about 0.31 s to each 25 s clip on this Mac.

Live text with Nemotron Unified.

With Nemotron Unified, Hushpen shows words while you speak. The first words showed 1.88 s after speech started, and the final text was ready 0.18 s after speech ended.

  • 1.88 sfrom the start of speech to the first words on screen.
  • 0.18 sfrom the end of speech to the final text.
  • 40text updates during a 25 s clip.

Three clips, three runs each, played in at microphone pace in 20 ms pieces. Times leave out model load, cleanup and pasting. Median of the three clip medians.
