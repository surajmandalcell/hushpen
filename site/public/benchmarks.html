We timed 6 dictation apps. Here's how Hushpen did.

Same 36 clips of real people talking, same Mac, every app. Updated 20 November 2026.

Pick any Hushpen model, from a 5 second note to a 30 minute meeting. It beats MacWhisper, VoiceInk and Buzz every time. To be fair, FluidVoice is quicker than us on anything over 5 seconds. It does 30 minutes in 8.5 s, 2.0x quicker than Hushpen with the same Parakeet model.

  1. Nemotron Unified is the default. It types while you talk, so the last words land 0.30 s after you stop. Give it a recording instead and it does 1 minute in 0.80 s.
  2. Buzz starts itself and loads its model again for every file. That's about 13 s each time.

  4. With the language left on Detect, Small and Base wrote Welsh or Korean on 6 and 8 of 36 clips.

Which Hushpen model should you use?

  1. For everyday dictation
    Nemotron

    0.30 s and your text is there

    Gets 94.5 of every 100 words right, and types while you talk. It's the default, so you don't have to touch a thing.

  2. When every word counts
    Turbo

    95.0 words right out of 100

    Only 0.1 off the top score. It does 30 minutes in 64.8 s. MacWhisper needs 95.5 s with the same model.

  3. For long meetings
    Parakeet v3

    17.0 s for a 30 minute meeting

    Beats Handy from 5 minutes up, and Handy gave no text at all on 30 minutes. Nemotron Unified takes 23.1 s. FluidVoice is quicker here, at 8.5 s. Parakeet gets 93.6 of 100 words right.
