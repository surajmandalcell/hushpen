# Site: benchmark content

Design picks for benchmark content on the public site (`site/public`).
The owner picked these from the options page
<https://claude.ai/artifact/QHcs2Bz7oopzqpjfnpBcCi>.

## Naming

- A Hushpen entry is "Hushpen + <model>", for example "Hushpen + Whisper Turbo".
  Hushpen entries are black and bold. Other apps are gray and underlined.
- Numbers come from our own runs only. A paid app with no run says "not
  tested yet"; vendor claims never appear as our numbers.

## Home page (`index.html`)

- Nav: "Benchmarks" sits just before "FAQ".
- Speed card: a gray link with a dotted underline, bottom left, "See the
  benchmarks →".
- Free item: the h3 is "Free forever. Open source." The small label is
  "AGPL-3.0 license".
- Hero: the title stays "Free dictation for Mac, Windows and Linux." The line
  under it is "You talk, Hushpen types it into any app. No subscription. No
  account."
- Compare card: a full-width card in the Why section, below the free item.
  It is a line chart: seconds to write out a recording (y) against its length
  (x, 5 s to 30 min), one line per app and Hushpen model with real Mac data.
  Short plain names only ("Hushpen Parakeet", "Hushpen Turbo", "FluidVoice",
  "Handy"). Each point is the median of 3 runs with the fastest and slowest
  run shown. The title is a plain sentence ending "Lower is better."
  The chart is generated by the bench script `home_chart.py` into the
  `<!-- cmp-chart -->` markers in `index.html`.

## Benchmarks page (`benchmarks.html`)

- Nav: the home nav, with "Benchmarks" lit by the white highlight. Same
  width as home (`.wrap`, 1184 px column). SEO pages use the same nav.
- Top: a short title and three numbers, no long paragraph.
- Results: three best-pick rows (most accurate, fast and accurate,
  balanced), each with the best other app beside it.
- Accent table: a shaded grid, darker cells for more words right.
- The "On this Mac" speed section is removed.
- Method: one closed line that links to `benchmarks-method.html`.

## Method page (`benchmarks-method.html`)

Laid out like a short research paper: abstract, setup, consistency rules
(warm model, load time excluded, median of runs, same clips for every app,
no tuning on test clips), results, limits, and exact steps to reproduce.
