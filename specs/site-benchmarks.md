# Site: benchmark content

Design picks for benchmark content on the public site (`site/public`).
The owner picked these from the options page
<https://claude.ai/artifact/QHcs2Bz7oopzqpjfnpBcCi>.

## Naming

- A Hushpen entry is "Hushpen + <model>", for example "Hushpen + Whisper Turbo".
  Hushpen entries are black and bold. Other apps are gray and underlined.
- Numbers come from our own runs only. A paid app with no run says "not
  tested yet"; vendor claims never appear as our numbers.

## Voice (all copy on the home, benchmarks, method and guide pages)

- Owner rule: every line sounds like a good salesperson talking across a
  table, in the same voice as the win lines. Short, warm, confident and
  specific. Lead with the number when there is one ("0.30 s after you stop
  talking, your text is there").
- No bot phrasing: no lab labels ("Text in 0.30 s after you stop"), no
  slogans that say nothing ("Measured, not claimed"), and no method words
  ("middle of 3 runs") in a heading. Method details go in a lead line, a
  footnote, or the method page, in plain words.
- The method page may stay calmer and more factual, but it still sounds like
  a person ("The short version", "Run it yourself"), not a lab report.
- Every claim stays true, and the bench script asserts it. Never "fastest".
  FluidVoice stays in every table, and the copy says plainly where it is
  quicker ("To be fair, FluidVoice is quicker than us on long files").

## Home page (`index.html`)

- Nav: "Benchmarks" sits just before "FAQ".
- Performance section: the title is "Best-in-class performance.", with a
  blue link "See the benchmarks for yourself →" on its right (under it on a
  phone). No win lines on home. Under it (owner pick): three tiles "4.1x,
  4.0x, 2.9x faster than MacWhisper / VoiceInk / Buzz", each with its proof line ("23.1 s
  against 95.5 s on 30 minutes of talk"), written by `site_page.py` into the
  `<!-- perf-tiles -->` markers; one row at desktop, stacked rows on a phone.
  Then the compare card at the same width.
- Free item: the h3 is "Free forever. Open source." The small label is
  "AGPL-3.0 license".
- Hero: the title stays "Free dictation for Mac, Windows and Linux." The line
  under it is "Just talk. Hushpen types it into any app. Free, with no account
  and no subscription."
- Homebrew line (owner pick, "Plain sentence"): under every big Download
  button (home hero, home end, the 7 guide pages) one line "Or with Homebrew:"
  then `brew install --cask samhqapp/tap/hushpen` on a thin gray chip with a
  copy icon. A click anywhere on the line copies; the icon turns to a check
  and a live region says "Copied". Centered under centered buttons, left under
  left ones; hidden on Windows and Linux. Styles: `.brew` in `kit.css`.
- Compare card: a full-width card in the performance section, under the
  tiles, with its title and "Lower is better." line inside the card. It is a
  line chart: seconds to write out a recording (y) against its length (x, 5 s
  to 30 min), one line per app and Hushpen model with real Mac data. No end
  labels and no leader lines: a legend row names each line
  with its swatch, Hushpen Nemotron first (bold blue, from the batch runs in
  `bench/unified-results.json`), then "Other Hushpen models" (one faint blue
  entry), MacWhisper, VoiceInk, Buzz, FluidVoice and Handy. Key points are
  the median of 3 runs. No vertical marks. The legend sits under the chart, above the caption.
  The caption is two short lines: the machine (MacBook Pro with M4 Pro, all
  apps on the Mac) and each app's model; asserts check both against the bench
  data. Longer notes (Handy, FluidVoice, Buzz start-up) live in the
  benchmarks footnotes. The chart is generated by the bench script
  `home_chart.py` into the `<!-- cmp-chart -->` markers in `index.html`.
- Hushpen Parakeet batch speed comes from `bench/mac_parakeet_fast/`
  (after e0be89be and dc20a811; 5 s, 1, 5 and 30 min only). Lengths it did
  not time again are left out, never mixed with the older build.

## Win lines

- Picked by the owner: rolling lines on the benchmarks top (one row of all
  eight, under the three numbers, 30 px, 80 s per loop) only. Never on home.
  Each line is a short headline with the number as proof in a small gray
  line under it. `site_page.py` writes them; `quotes.css` styles them.
- Every headline claim has an assert in `site_page.py`. No line may say or
  imply Hushpen is the fastest; FluidVoice is faster. Plain facts about other apps,
  never mean.
- Owner rule: the number leads the headline, said like a person selling across
  the table. Speed headlines start "<N>x faster than <App>", where N is rounded
  down to one decimal and always shown with it ("2.9x", "4.0x").
  The small proof names the length used: "<N>x on <5, 10 or 30> min of talk: Hushpen
  <s>, <App> <s>". N is Hushpen's default (Nemotron Unified, whole file)
  against the app, at the SMALLEST ratio over 5, 10 and 30 min (owner: no inflated claims); an assert checks
  it. Files under 5 min are never used (Buzz spends about 13 s on app start). Never
  compare with FluidVoice.
- The lines today (owner's wording; the numbers are generated from the data):
  1. 4.1x faster than MacWhisper. Not a typo. (30 min: 23.1 s, 95.5 s)
  2. Stop talking. Your words are already there. (0.30 s, default model)
  3. 4.0x faster than VoiceInk. You'll feel it. (30 min: 23.1 s, 94.2 s)
  4. Gets 94 of every 100 words right. Accent and all. (94.5 of 100, 19
     English accents)
  5. 2.9x faster than Buzz. Grab a coffee? No time. (30 min: 23.1 s, 69.0 s)
  6. Same model as MacWhisper. Still 1.4x quicker. (Whisper Turbo, 30 min:
     64.8 s, 95.5 s)
  7. Handy stops at 30 minutes. Hushpen keeps going. (30 min: Handy no text
     in 3 of 3 runs, Hushpen 23.1 s)
- The rows start on the page text edge (left padding equals the page margin),
  pause on hover, and with Reduce Motion become still rows you can swipe.

## Benchmarks page (`benchmarks.html`)

Owner pick 2026-11-19: direction B "Leaderboard" from
<https://claude.ai/artifact/N67MxbFDvNk3ZeHS8Nh77j>. Generated by the bench
script `site_page.py`; styles in `bench.css`, sorting and the hover card in
`bench.js`.

- Nav: the home nav, with "Benchmarks" lit. No visible breadcrumb (the
  JSON-LD breadcrumb stays). The 7 guide pages (compare.css) use the same
  nav, the 1240 px width, left-aligned 40 px titles and 24 px section titles.
- Table and grid headers show the short model name ("Nemotron", "Parakeet
  v3", "Turbo") on one line at 1440. The full name goes in the hover card
  and footnotes.
- Top: title "We timed 6 dictation apps. Here's how Hushpen did.", one plain
  line, a "See how we tested →"
  link (accent color at lower opacity, dotted underline). Under it, the three
  numbers sit in one white card (owner pick B, round 2): number left, label
  and "Higher/Lower is better" right, hairlines between them; on a phone the
  three stack as rows. The numbers are Nemotron Unified's words right and
  wait after you stop, then Parakeet v3's 30 minute time, named as such.
- Nemotron Unified is the headline Hushpen model (the default for new
  installs; existing installs keep Parakeet v3). Its words right, accents and
  file speeds come from the batch call in `bench/unified-results.json`.
- Order: leaderboard, our picks, wait after you stop talking, by accent,
  claims, method link and date.
- Leaderboard ("Every app, side by side"): every app and model, best first,
  with a switch (Speed, the default; Accuracy; Long meetings) and sortable headers (`aria-sort`).
  Model names carry no "(default)" label in any chart or table; footnote 1
  says Nemotron Unified is the default. It types while you talk, so its
  1 minute cell says "Real time" with a gray dash in the seconds slot, and
  sorts first; its hover card and footnote give its real final wait (0.30 s). Its 30 minute cell is the batch time. Only
  the sorted column draws bars; Hushpen bars are black, others gray. Gaps
  show a dash with a footnote, never "Not measured". Numbered footnotes under
  the table say what each time includes (Nemotron through live text, Buzz
  loads the model per file, who skipped 30 minutes, Small and Base wrong
  language).
- Blue tint: a Hushpen row or column that sits beside other apps has the
  soft tint `rgba(0, 110, 240, .065)` with a very faint white sheen (top to
  bottom on rows, a center streak down columns), the method page results
  table too.
  The wait table holds only Hushpen, so it has no tint.
- Picks ("Which Hushpen model should you use?"): three cards: everyday
  dictation (Nemotron Unified, the default), when every word counts (Turbo),
  long meetings (Parakeet v3, asserted
  faster than Nemotron Unified at 30 minutes). Never call Hushpen "most accurate" unless its score is the top
  one; say how far behind and that it is faster, and the faster claim must
  hold at 1 and 30 minutes (asserted in `site_page.py`).
- Wait numbers ("Stop talking. How long until your text is there?") from
  `bench/e2e-results.json` (full app, WAV fed at real time, speech end until
  text is in TextEdit, middle of 3 warm runs; ranges show the run count).
  Only Hushpen is in that table.
- Accent grid: Hushpen Nemotron Unified first (it sets the row order), then
  Turbo and Parakeet v3, then every other app. Score bands on the shown
  rounded scores: the best in each row green and bold, 90 and up plain (a
  good score), 80 to 89 amber, below 80 red. The key lists only the bands in
  use. The three Hushpen columns carry the tint.
- App labels: the app name, then the short model name inline ("Hushpen
  Turbo"). Every app name opens one shared hover card (what it is, model
  tested, price, where it runs; facts checked on each app's own site). The
  card is 380 px, a blurred white material with a soft layered shadow. Mouse:
  hover. Touch: tap opens, tap outside closes. Keyboard: focus.
- Claims: a table titled "They say it. We checked it." (tested apps), then a
  "Haven't tested these yet" list. No "verified" or fancy wording.
- Words: short, plain sentences. Body text 16-17 px, line height 1.55, a
  measure of 60-75 characters; calm titles (h1 40 px, h2 24 px) with clear
  space between title, line and content.

## Method page (`benchmarks-method.html`)

Laid out like a short research paper: abstract, setup, consistency rules
(warm model, load time excluded, median of runs, same clips for every app,
no tuning on test clips), results, limits, and exact steps to reproduce.
No visible breadcrumb. Apps not timed after you stop talking show a dash.
Same site width as home and benchmarks (1240 px); prose keeps a 68-72
character measure.
`/benchmarks-method` works live (Cloudflare drops `.html`) and locally with
`python3 site/preview.py`.
